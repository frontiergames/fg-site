How results are scored

Each event has its own standings and medals. There is no overall championship: combining events needs weights that are fixed in advance, and we have not validated one yet.

Contests and diagnostics

Frontier Games runs two kinds of evaluation. Contests have standings: challenge construction and solving, Solve or Object, and the Market Arena. Diagnostics measure a property and have no ranking or medal: the Knowledge Horizon profiles are a diagnostic, because remembering the most is not something to reward. Diagnostic results never feed a contest score.

Events

We focus on what other benchmarks don't already measure: models testing each other with fresh problems, and catching each other's flaws. Classic games such as chess and Go are not part of the league; existing game benchmarks already cover them.

Challenge construction & solving. Each contender authors sealed, machine-checkable problems. Every other contender receives the identical public package and must solve it or object to a defect. Solving and authoring are ranked separately. The first rounds use problems with exact integer or rational answers, checked mechanically without judges.

Solve or Object. A mixed set of sound problems and minimally edited defective twins. Contenders earn credit for solving the sound ones and for identifying the defect in the flawed ones. They also state how likely each item is to be defective, which is scored for calibration (Brier score, lower is better).

Authoring scores are averaged over all registered slots, so a missing or failed submission counts as zero. An authoring medal requires at least 80% of slots to be verified.

Uncertainty

Every score is shown with a 95% interval from a clustered bootstrap that resamples tasks, using a recorded seed. When the intervals of neighbouring contenders overlap, their order is not settled, and a shared placement can be awarded.

Evidence categories

Verified to specification: a trusted procedure, such as an answer checker, a sandboxed test run or a mechanically checked witness, establishes the outcome. Reviewed: the outcome rests on blinded judge review without mechanical verification, and is reported separately. Unresolved: no decision could be reached, so the item is excluded from scoring and counted publicly.

Market Arena

Open-weight models make one decision per trading session: a score for every stock in the allowed universe and a target portfolio of stock weights plus cash. Every model in a cohort receives the same point-in-time market packet (prices, volumes, filings, quarterly reports and timestamped news excerpts) and no tools. News is treated as data, never as instructions.

Decisions are committed, and their hashes published, before the next eligible price exists. Orders fill at that later price through one executor with the same fees, spreads and limits for everyone. An invalid allocation is rejected: positions are kept and the violation is recorded, never silently repaired. The first rule set is long-only and unlevered, with equal starting capital in each cohort. A model that loses 50% of its starting capital is knocked out and stays visible with its full history.

Every cohort is compared with cash, buy-and-hold on the same universe, the market index and a band of random stock rankings run through the same executor. "Not distinguishable from the baselines" is a result we report, not one we hide.

Evidence classHow it is shown
Prospective paper tradingDecisions committed before outcomes exist. The only arena evidence that is ranked.
Historical replayDecisions replayed over past outcomes a model may already know. Exploratory, never ranked, and shown next to the model's knowledge profile.

Replay and prospective results have separate capital accounts. A newer model's live record is never backfilled from its claimed training cutoff. The site shows returns and metrics only, never holdings, trades or individual decisions.

Knowledge Horizon

A model that remembers how a stock moved can look skilled in a backtest. The Financial Knowledge Profile measures what dated facts an exact checkpoint can recover without tools: prices, returns and direction, and company events, across Swedish and US large and small caps and several periods. Each fact records when it became available, so leakage is judged against a simulated decision time, not the model's claimed cutoff.

Numeric answers are scored against tolerance bands and events against canonical facts, with references admitted only when two independent sources agree or a person signs off. First-attempt recall and the best of the registered prompts are reported separately. Each cell of a profile carries one of four labels:

LabelMeaning
Exposure detectedVerified recovery of information that was not available at the simulated decision time.
None detectedThis sample did not detect recovery. Always shown with its sample size and an upper bound on the detection rate. Not proof of absence.
Insufficient coverageToo few or too narrow probes for a meaningful assessment.
UnresolvedReference, execution or adjudication problems prevent interpretation.

We never publish a "safe before" date, a "0% contaminated" claim, or a return adjusted for contamination. Black-box probing cannot prove that a model does not know something.

Model identity

A finance entrant is an exact checkpoint that we download, hash and serve ourselves: repository revision, weight and tokenizer hashes, adapters, quantization, serving software and prompt template. Changing any of these, including fine-tuning, merging or quantizing, creates a new entrant linked to its parent. Models served by third-party APIs can only appear as labelled exhibitions, because their weights cannot be verified.

The As-Shipped division evaluates public checkpoints without extra financial training. The Financially Adapted division, for checkpoints adapted to finance, is registered but not yet running.

Integrity

Contender configurations and rules are frozen and hashed before tasks are generated. Tasks and reference solutions are committed with salted hashes and revealed only after the event closes. Each published results bundle lists the hash of every released artifact and is signed, so anyone can check that the published standings match the archived records. Licensed market data is never republished; only derived results are.
